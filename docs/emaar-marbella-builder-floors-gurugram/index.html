Inside Emaar Marbella: tree-lined internal roads

The internal roads of Emaar Marbella, the gated villa community in Sector 65-66 where our four independent builder floors are being built, are quiet and green. The concrete lanes are lined with clipped hedges and young trees, and the new residential towers going up along Golf Course Extension Road can be seen beyond the community boundary, a sign of how quickly this part of Gurugram is growing around Marbella's quiet, low-rise streets. Follow the build on our Emaar Marbella construction progress page.

Four independent builder floors in Emaar Marbella, Sector 65-66, Gurugram

Bhutani Realtors is offering four independent floors in a new building on Plot 21, Emaar Marbella Phase-II, a gated villa community on Golf Course Extension Road (Sectors 65-66), Gurugram. There is one home per floor, with a basement, stilt parking and a private car-park lane, on a park-facing corner plot next to Golf Estate. Construction is under way.

Why Emaar Marbella

Emaar Marbella is a gated, landscaped Emaar community with lamp-lit, tree-lined avenues and villas. A new residents' club for the Marbella community is under construction on Golf Course Extension Road, and residents also have access to the existing Marbella Phase-1 villas club. Emaar continues to invest in the estate, with Marbella Terraces in Sector 66 listed as a new 2026 launch (HRERA Gurugram registration GM/1068/800/2026/40). The community sits next to Golf Estate, close to Gurugram's growing corporate and office corridor, which makes it a natural address for senior professionals and business owners who want a quiet, secure neighbourhood within easy reach of work.

Green, quiet and home to peacocks

The photos at the top of this page were taken inside Emaar Marbella, Sector 65, Golf Course Extension Road: sculpted topiary, wide lawns and shaded lanes between the villas, and the peacocks that roam the community's gardens. It is the kind of everyday calm that is hard to find this close to Gurugram's office towers. To explore the wider neighbourhood, read our Golf Course Extension Road area guide.
