NRI Property Management in Gurugram

One accountable local team for your Gurugram home while you live abroad.

Your Gurugram home, looked after while you're abroad

Owning a home in Gurugram while living in Dubai, London, Singapore or Toronto usually means relying on a relative, a broker or a society guard to keep an eye on it. NRI Property Care from Bhutani Realtors gives you one accountable local team instead: we find and check tenants, keep track of the rent, pay the taxes and dues, coordinate repairs, and send you a short report every month.

What we take care of

  • Tenant search and screening: listing the home, showing it, checking the tenant's background and employment, and shortlisting for your approval.
  • Rent agreement and police verification: the rent agreement for your approval and signature, and tenant police verification with Haryana Police before the keys are handed over.
  • Rent tracking and reminders: a rent calendar for your home, reminders to the tenant before each due date, follow-ups when rent is late, and a monthly statement showing what was received.
  • Tenant TDS: a tenant paying rent to an NRI landlord must deduct tax at source and file the related forms. We give every tenant a simple guide, remind them each month, and collect their TDS certificates for your records.
  • Property tax, society dues and bills: paid on time from a small prepaid amount you keep with us, with every receipt filed and shared.
  • Inspections and repairs: regular visits with photos and video, repairs through vetted local vendors at actual cost, and a move-out inspection when a tenancy ends.
  • Lock-and-leave care: for homes that are vacant or used only on visits, monthly checks, cleaning before you arrive, and key custody.

How we work

  • Rent goes straight to you. The tenant pays directly into your NRO account. We never collect or hold your rent.
  • Nothing happens without your approval. Tenant choice, agreement terms and any repair above an agreed limit are confirmed with you first.
  • One point of contact on WhatsApp, and a written management agreement with a specific authority letter for each task, never a general power of attorney.
  • Original documents stay with you. We work from copies and keep a secure digital file of your property papers.

Plans

  • Essential Care: for vacant or self-use homes. A fixed yearly fee for monthly inspections, bill and dues payments, and key care.
  • Rent Manager: for rented homes. A share of the monthly rent, charged only in months the home is let, covering tenant management, rent tracking, inspections and repairs.
  • Signature: for multiple properties or owners who want closer attention, with monthly inspections, video walk-throughs and a yearly summary for your chartered accountant.

Frequently asked questions

Do you collect rent on my behalf? No. The tenant pays rent directly into your bank account. We track it, remind the tenant, and report to you each month.

Does my tenant need to deduct tax from the rent? Yes. Rent paid to a non-resident landlord is subject to tax deduction at source, whatever the amount, and the tenant must file the related forms. We remind the tenant each month and collect the certificates; a chartered accountant handles the filings and any lower-deduction certificate for you.

Is tenant police verification required in Gurugram? Yes. Tenant verification is required by Haryana Police and can be done online. We complete it before the tenant moves in.

Which areas do you cover? Gurugram, with a focus on Golf Course Extension Road, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, Dwarka Expressway and New Gurugram.

What if there is a dispute with the tenant? We try to resolve it directly with the tenant and keep you informed. If legal action is needed, you are free to appoint a lawyer of your choice, and we will share all records with them.
