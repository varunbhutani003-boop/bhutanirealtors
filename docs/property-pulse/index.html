Gurugram Property Pulse — 09 Nov 2026: A ₹325-Crore Cyber City Flyover, a Sector 59 Launch on Track and Global Luxury Retail Arrives

1. DLF awards ₹325 crore contract for a new Cyber City flyover. DLF has awarded a contract worth about ₹325 crore for a flyover from the Sirhaul border towards Cyber City, alongside the Delhi–Gurugram Expressway, with four underpasses linking Moulsari Avenue to the Delhi-bound carriageway and to DLF developments. The project has Haryana government and NHAI approval; building is planned over about 18 months once the traffic management plan is cleared. Source: Construction World, 9 Nov 2026

2. Max Estates: Sector 59 Gurugram launch on track for this quarter. On its 5 Nov earnings call, Max Estates said its Sector 59 Gurugram launch remains on track for the October–December quarter. The developer reported first-half FY27 pre-sales of about ₹3,200 crore (Q2 alone about ₹2,100 crore across 274 homes) and a growth pipeline of about ₹29,000 crore in development value. Sources: GuruFocus earnings-call highlights, 7 Nov 2026; Sahi, 6 Oct 2026

3. De Beers' Forevermark opens a store in Gurugram. Forevermark, the diamond jewellery brand of the De Beers Group, has opened a store at Gold Souk Mall, Sector 43, through franchise partner Vismay Jewels. The brand is targeting 20 stores in India by the end of 2026 and 100 by 2030, another sign of global luxury brands choosing Gurugram. Source: PropNewsTime, 9 Nov 2026

Why it matters for Golf Course Extension Road: Sector 59 sits on Golf Course Extension Road, so another listed developer launching there adds to the corridor's premium address, while new links into Cyber City make the office commute from the GCER side easier.

Gurugram Property Pulse — 07 Nov 2026: DLF Puts ₹3,750 Crore Behind IREO and a Golf Course Road Home Tops ₹140 Crore

1. DLF and Everstone sign up to invest in IREO. DLF and Singapore’s Everstone Group have signed binding term sheets to form a joint venture that will invest in IREO’s India companies, with DLF putting in about ₹3,750 crore for a majority stake in the JV. The JV will take at least 20% of the IREO companies (about 40% economic interest), and the partners cite development opportunities in Delhi, Gurugram and other regions; closing is expected in 2027, subject to approvals. Source: Business Standard, 6 Nov 2026

2. A Golf Course Road luxury home now estimated at ₹140 crore-plus. A 10,813 sq ft apartment at DLF The Camellias on Golf Course Road, bought by Zomato founder Deepinder Goyal for ₹52.3 crore in August 2022, could now fetch over ₹140 crore according to broker estimates — an indicative gain of about 168%. Source: Business Today, 5 Nov 2026

Why it matters for Golf Course Extension Road: India’s biggest listed developer committing fresh capital to Gurugram land, and strong top-end values on neighbouring Golf Course Road, both point to deep and lasting demand for premium homes in this part of the city. Gurugram Property Pulse — 06 Nov 2026: NCR Home Values Up 10%, Signature Global Swings to Profit and a 500-Bed Hospital for Gurugram

1. NCR home values up 10% as sales and launches keep climbing. JLL's latest residential data shows Delhi-NCR sold 30,242 homes in January–September 2026, up 8% year-on-year, while launches rose 9% to 33,032 homes and capital values were up 10% in the July–September quarter. Across the top seven cities, homes priced ₹1.5–3 crore were the fastest-growing segment, with sales up 54%. Source: BW Businessworld, 5 Nov 2026

2. Signature Global swings to a ₹70-crore profit as revenue jumps 148%. The Gurugram developer reported a Q2 FY27 net profit of ₹70 crore (against a ₹50 crore loss a year ago) on revenue of ₹820 crore. Its average sales realisation rose 12.3% to ₹17,122 per sq ft, helped by the Tonino Lamborghini Residences launch in Sector 71 on the Southern Peripheral Road, and collections grew 9% to ₹1,010 crore. Source: Business Standard, 5 Nov 2026

3. Manipal Hospitals plans a 500-bed multispecialty hospital in Gurugram. Manipal Health Enterprises has signed a 30-year lease with TIL Investments (part of Singapore-listed Thakral Corporation) for about 3.5 acres near IGI Airport, for a hospital of up to 500 beds in its first phase. CEO Dilip Jose called Delhi-NCR "an important market" for the group. Source: Digital Health News, 5 Nov 2026

Why it matters for Golf Course Extension Road: Rising NCR home values, stronger luxury pricing on the nearby SPR and new top-tier healthcare all strengthen the case for premium, low-density living around Golf Course Extension Road. Gurugram Property Pulse — 05 Nov 2026: A ₹3,500-Crore Landmark Tower on Golf Course Extension Road, New Flyovers and Fresh SPR Investment

1. Pioneer Urban unveils Alura, a ₹3,500-crore ultra-luxury address in Sector 62. On 3 November, Pioneer Urban launched Alura: about 250 serviced residences in two towers on 6.99 acres inside Pioneer District on Golf Course Extension Road, designed by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill, with an East Tower rising about 200 metres. The developer expects sales realisation of over ₹9,000 crore and is targeting completion in Q2 2031. Source: Business Standard, 3 Nov 2026

2. ₹136-crore package: two new flyovers and an underpass approved. A high-powered works purchase committee chaired by Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini cleared ₹136.41 crore of GMDA works: a six-lane flyover at Dadi Sati Chowk (Sectors 85–86/89–90), a four-lane flyover at Ambedkar Chowk on Vikas Marg, and an underpass with a covered foot overbridge near Millennium City Centre metro station. Each has an 18-month build period from award. Source: The Tribune, 3 Nov 2026

3. Signature Global adds land on the Southern Peripheral Road. Signature Global has acquired a further 1.1 acres in Sector 71 on the SPR, adding about 0.1 million sq ft of developable area to a 4.26-acre collaboration project. Source: Whalesbook, 3 Nov 2026

4. Kreeva lines up ₹5,000 crore for luxury homes, including an SPR project. After the first phase of its Delhi project Swaranaya was booked out on launch day, Kanodia Group's Kreeva said it will invest about ₹5,000 crore over four to five years across Delhi-NCR and select tier-2 cities, with a multigenerational housing project being prepared on Southern Peripheral Road, Gurugram. Source: PropNewsTime, 3 Nov 2026

Gurugram Property Pulse — 03 Nov 2026: A Signal-Free Sohna Corridor, Easier Expressway Commutes and Rising Buyer Demand

1. Centre clears a ₹340-crore elevated corridor at Sohna Chowk. Union Road Transport Minister Nitin Gadkari has announced approval for a 2.75 km, six-lane elevated corridor at the NH-919/NH-248A junction at Sohna Chowk, costing ₹340.78 crore. It will separate Gurugram–Nuh through traffic from local traffic for signal-free movement and improve links to the Delhi–Mumbai and KMP expressways. Source: The Tribune, 2 Nov 2026; Swarajya, 1 Nov 2026

2. Annual toll pass for local commuters starts 1 November. Under a scheme announced by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways on 27 Oct, owners of private vehicles living within 20 km of a toll plaza can now pay a fixed annual fee of ₹1,500 or ₹3,000 (depending on the plaza), a welcome saving for daily commuters on Dwarka Expressway via the Bijwasan plaza. The pass will also be available on the Rajmargyatra app from 1 December. Source: The Tribune, 31 Oct 2026

  1. Homebuyer demand climbs 8.2% as premium homes gain traction. Magicbricks' PropIndex for July–September 2026 shows housing demand across India up 8.2% quarter-on-quarter against 1.9% growth in supply, with premium homes drawing more buyer interest and 2 and 3 BHK homes accounting for the bulk of it. Source: The Realty Today, 27 Oct 2026

Why it matters for Golf Course Extension Road: Faster, signal-free links towards Sohna and the expressways, cheaper daily highway commutes and stronger buyer demand for premium homes all support Gurugram's southern growth belt, which Sectors 58–67 anchor. Gurugram Property Pulse — 02 Nov 2026: Lodha Arrives on Golf Course Road, NCR Prices Lead the Country and Offices Fill Fast

1. Lodha makes its NCR debut with a Grade A++ office tower on Golf Course Road. Mumbai's Lodha Developers has launched Lodha Pantheon, a 27-storey office project with about 4 lakh sq ft of saleable space on a 1.2-acre site, an investment of roughly ₹1,100–1,200 crore. The company says early customer response is good and it plans a residential launch on Sohna Road later this financial year. Source: Business Standard, 2 Nov 2026

2. NCR records the fastest price growth among India's top seven cities. Anarock data cited this festive season show NCR home prices up 12% year-on-year in July–September, ahead of every other major city. Emaar India's CEO Kalyan Chakrabarti pointed to good footfall at the company's Gurugram projects and rising interest in the ₹3–6 crore segment. Source: The Tribune, 2 Nov 2026

3. A new 600-seat workspace opens half-leased on day one. Innov8 has opened a 30,000 sq ft managed office centre at Well Done Tech Park, with 50% of its capacity already taken at launch. The centre is linked to Sohna Road, Golf Course Extension Road and the Southern Peripheral Road, and is Innov8's tenth in Gurugram. Source: PropNewsTime, 1 Nov 2026

4. Godrej Properties lines up Sector 63A in its second-half launch pipeline. Godrej Properties has listed a Sector 63A, Gurugram project among launches in its roughly ₹32,000-crore pipeline for the second half of FY27, and management described early signals from the Golf Course Extension Road micro-market as encouraging. Source: Godrej Properties Q2 FY27 earnings commentary, 27 Oct 2026

Gurugram Property Pulse — 1 Nov 2026: A ₹15,000-Crore Landmark Plan, a Lamborghini Build Contract and Metro Phase 2 Bids
