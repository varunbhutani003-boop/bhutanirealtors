Golf Course Extension Road, Gurugram: Area Guide

Connectivity, conveniences and what is being built along one of Gurugram's fastest-growing corridors.

Where is Golf Course Extension Road?

Golf Course Extension Road (GCER) is one of Gurugram's main residential and commercial corridors. It sits between Golf Course Road, NH-48 and Sohna Road, and links to the Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), giving residents routes towards Cyber City, Udyog Vihar and the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway.

Connectivity

Roads

GCER connects with Golf Course Road and the Southern Peripheral Road. An elevated corridor on SPR, from Vatika Chowk towards NH-48, is at the tender stage, which is expected to ease travel across south Gurugram.

Metro

The Rapid Metro station at Sector 55–56 serves the area today. A Gurugram metro extension from Sector 56 towards Pachgaon has been proposed along this belt.

Everyday conveniences nearby

  • Schools: DPS International, The Shriram Millennium School, RPS International School
  • Hospitals: CK Birla Hospital, Artemis Hospital
  • Shopping: Airia Mall and other retail along the corridor
  • Offices: business parks such as Worldmark and AIPL Business Club

New developments on Golf Course Extension Road

The corridor keeps attracting big-ticket commercial investment. Landmark Group has announced a Rs 4,000 crore Grade A+ corporate office development on Golf Course Extension Road (Hindustan Times, 30 Oct 2026), and DLF has been adding land next to its Sector 61 site. Godrej Properties has also listed a Sector 63A project in its second-half FY27 launch pipeline (company earnings commentary, 27 Oct 2026), and Lodha Developers has launched Lodha Pantheon, a Grade A++ office tower on nearby Golf Course Road (Business Standard, 2 Nov 2026). To the south, the Centre has approved a Rs 340.78 crore, six-lane elevated corridor at Sohna Chowk for signal-free travel towards Sohna and the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway (The Tribune, 2 Nov 2026). On the residential side, Pioneer Urban has unveiled Alura, a Rs 3,500 crore ultra-luxury serviced-residences project of about 250 homes designed by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill in Sector 62 on Golf Course Extension Road (Business Standard, 3 Nov 2026), and the state has approved Rs 136.41 crore for two new flyovers and an underpass across Gurugram (The Tribune, 3 Nov 2026). Market data is supportive too: JLL reports Delhi-NCR home sales up 8% and capital values up 10% year-on-year (BW Businessworld, 5 Nov 2026), and Manipal Hospitals plans a multispecialty hospital of up to 500 beds in Gurugram (Digital Health News, 5 Nov 2026). Big developers are also putting fresh capital into Gurugram: DLF and Everstone have signed binding term sheets for a joint venture to invest in IREO's India companies, with DLF committing about Rs 3,750 crore (Business Standard, 6 Nov 2026). On neighbouring Golf Course Road, brokers estimate a 10,813 sq ft apartment at DLF The Camellias bought for Rs 52.3 crore in 2022 could now fetch over Rs 140 crore (Business Today, 5 Nov 2026). Max Estates has said its Sector 59 Gurugram launch, on Golf Course Extension Road, remains on track for the October-December 2026 quarter (earnings call, 5 Nov 2026), and DLF has awarded a Rs 325 crore contract for a new flyover from the Sirhaul border into Cyber City, Gurugram's main office hub (Construction World, 9 Nov 2026).
