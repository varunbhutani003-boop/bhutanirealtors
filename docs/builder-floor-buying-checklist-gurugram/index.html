Buying a Builder Floor in Gurugram: A Checklist

Documents and practical points to check before you commit.

Buying a builder floor in Gurugram: what to check

An independent builder floor is one home per floor in a low-rise building on a private plot. Builder floors are a popular choice in Gurugram's plotted communities, including Emaar Marbella on Golf Course Extension Road. Because each floor is sold separately, buyers should check a few documents and practical points before committing. This checklist is general information for home buyers, not legal or financial advice.

1. Title and ownership

  • Ask for the chain of title documents for the plot and check that the seller's name matches the records.
  • Ask whether there is any existing loan or charge on the property and how it will be cleared before the sale.

2. Approvals for the building

  • Ask to see the sanctioned building plan for the plot and confirm that what is being built matches it.
  • For a completed building, ask for the occupation certificate issued after construction.
  • Check whether the property needs registration with the Haryana Real Estate Regulatory Authority (HRERA) and, if it does, verify the registration on the HRERA website.

3. What exactly you are buying

  • Confirm in writing which floor you are buying, the share in the land that comes with it, and your rights over the terrace, basement, stilt parking and common areas.
  • Ask how parking is allotted between the floors.

4. Community and running costs

  • In a gated community, ask about the maintenance agency, monthly charges, club access and security arrangements.
  • Visit at different times of day to see access roads, traffic and the neighbourhood.

5. Paperwork at the time of purchase

  • An agreement to sell that records the payment schedule, specifications and timelines in writing.
  • Registration of the sale deed with the sub-registrar and payment of stamp duty.
  • Transfer of the property in municipal and community records after registration.
