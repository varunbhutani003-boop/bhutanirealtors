# Golf Course Extension Road, Gurugram: Area Guide

## Where is Golf Course Extension Road?

Golf Course Extension Road (GCER) is one of Gurugram's main residential and commercial corridors. It sits between Golf Course Road, NH-48 and Sohna Road, and links to the Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), giving residents routes towards Cyber City, Udyog Vihar and the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway.

## Connectivity

### Roads

GCER connects with Golf Course Road and the Southern Peripheral Road. An elevated corridor on SPR, from Vatika Chowk towards NH-48, is at the tender stage, which is expected to ease travel across south Gurugram.

### Metro

The Rapid Metro station at Sector 55–56 serves the area today. A Gurugram metro extension from Sector 56 towards Pachgaon has been proposed along this belt.

## Everyday conveniences nearby
- Schools: DPS International, The Shriram Millennium School, RPS International School
- Hospitals: CK Birla Hospital, Artemis Hospital
- Shopping: Airia Mall and other retail along the corridor
- Offices: business parks such as Worldmark and AIPL Business Club
